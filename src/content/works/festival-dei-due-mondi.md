**Evocation of Spirits** was made for the 69th Festival dei Due Mondi, and it is the first time
a complete work of mine has been shown in Italy.

A festival that has run since 1958 is made of people who are no longer there. Composers, dancers,
directors, audiences — everyone who passed through Spoleto and left something behind that cannot
be hung on a wall. The edition was built around the theme of roots, and this is where the work
begins: not with the architecture, but with what the architecture has absorbed.

Seven of the city's landmark palazzi become the surface. Faces built by machine, affective
geometries, psychological landscapes — projected onto stone that has been standing for centuries.
The buildings are not the subject. They are the place where the work is shown, the way a wall in
a museum is the place where a painting is shown.

Alongside the facades, the Ex Museo Civico holds a digital exhibition of the same work,
shown during the day **to seven people at a time**. Seven viewers, seven palazzi. The intimacy
is the point: the same material that covers a whole square at night is met almost privately
by day.

The festival's visual arts programme for this edition also featured Giuseppe Penone, who made
the official poster for the 69th, with a city-wide exhibition in collaboration with Gagosian.

On 9 July I presented *Glitches & Glory* at the Giardini di Palazzo Campello, in conversation
with Chiara Ribichini of Sky TG24.
