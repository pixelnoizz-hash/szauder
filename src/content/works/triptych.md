Conceived for Saint Benoît Church, David Szauder’s *Triptych: Memory, Doubt, and Fates* brings
together three interconnected bodies of work around memory, self-confrontation, and collective
voice. Spanning the church’s three spaces, the installation combines *Failed Memories*,
*Doppelgänger*, and *Folkloric Robots* developed in collaboration with Anna Réka Baktay to trace
what endures from past to present through light, image, and artificial intelligence.
